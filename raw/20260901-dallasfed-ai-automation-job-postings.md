# Job postings show early signs of AI automation impact

> Canonical source: https://www.dallasfed.org/research/economics/2026/0901

## Source locator

- Published 2026-09-01.
- Uses occupation-level GenAI automation exposure derived from Anthropic task/Claude usage measures.
- Links exposure to Lightcast online job postings.
- Lightcast covers more than 220,000 online job boards and company sites, but underrepresents farming, construction, maintenance and personal-service openings.
- Within-industry comparison:
  - a 10-percentage-point difference in automatable-task exposure is associated with postings about 5% lower by end-2023;
  - about 8% lower by 2025 Q1.
- Balanced panel of incumbent firms:
  - more-exposed firms reduce postings around 5–6% by mid-2024;
  - around 8–9% by early 2026.
- Composition shifts away from automatable occupations within firms.
- Authors estimate total Texas Lightcast postings reduced by about:
  - 1.8% in 2024;
  - 2.6% in 2025.

## Evidence boundary

Job postings are labor-demand signals, not actual hires, employment stock or layoffs. Lightcast coverage is selective across occupations. Exposure is occupation/task-level rather than directly observed firm AI expenditure. The article reports strong associations and explicitly frames them as early labor-demand evidence, not a complete causal accounting of employment outcomes.
