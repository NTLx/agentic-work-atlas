# Prompting Change

> Canonical source: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/events/2026/s-darougheh-slides.pdf
> Evidence status: public conference slides, not a full paper.

## Source locator

- Slides dated 2026-06-24, BoE CCBS.
- Links 2023/2024/2025 Danish firm IT Usage Survey to 2018–2025 monthly employer–employee administrative records.
- Descriptive matched sample:
  - 4,468 firms;
  - 746,802 employees.
- Population frame focuses on firms with at least 10 employees.
- Design compares firms first adopting in 2023 with never-adopters using survey weights, industry/size/time controls and pre-adoption trends.
- Outcomes include log FTE employment, hourly wages, quarterly hiring and separation.
- Public slides report adopter FTE roughly 8% below relative pre-trend, with adjustment concentrated among workers under 30 and primarily via lower hiring.

## Evidence boundary

FTE is not headcount. Adoption is not randomized and event-study controls do not remove all selection. The public slides do not disclose enough detail to fully reproduce linkage keys, occupation crosswalk, exact H/S definitions or the complete end date. The public result set does not include training completion or independent skill outcomes.
