# Illustrating the Gemini App

> Canonical source: https://design.google/library/gemini-ai-visual-design

## Source locator

- Official Google Design feature on the Gemini app design system.
- Describes Gemini as a constantly evolving AI assistant for which traditional linear, predictable and control-rooted design methods are insufficient.
- Current Gemini design goals include:
  - intuitive;
  - immersive;
  - approachable;
  - aspirational;
  - trustworthy.
- Designers use gradients, responsive containers and motion as contextual cues.
- Motion is used to make the system's activity legible:
  - thinking;
  - analysis;
  - listening;
  - information synthesis;
  - directional progress.
- Design lead Anna Sera Garcia describes a nonlinear ideation process and continually changing deliverables.
- The team emphasizes a foundational experience that:
  - forgives mistakes;
  - anticipates confusion;
  - invites exploration;
  - communicates system state without overwhelming users.
- The piece frames the designer as a cartographer of a continuously evolving relational experience rather than a fixed tool.

## Evidence boundary

This is a current first-party Gemini design-team retrospective, but it is focused primarily on visual/interaction language rather than organization, launch process or model-eval ownership. It provides strong evidence that model state, changeability, trust and legibility are design objects. It does not provide performance metrics, RACI, incident outcomes, experiment velocity or responsibility handoff.
