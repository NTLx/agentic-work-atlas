# Businesses Are Using AI to Transform Work, Not Cut Jobs

> Canonical source: https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/09/businesses-are-using-ai-to-transform-work-not-cut-jobs/

## Source locator

- Survey field period: August 2026.
- Geography: New York and northern New Jersey regional business surveys.
- Pure information-search use is excluded from the AI-adoption definition.
- Among AI-using service firms, reported actions over the prior six months:
  - hired fewer workers: 15%;
  - hired more workers: 13%;
  - laid off workers: 4%;
  - retrained workers: 34%.
- Among AI-using manufacturers:
  - hired fewer: 6%;
  - hired more: 0%;
  - layoffs: 0%;
  - retraining: 22%.
- Official chart workbook provides marginal percentages, not firm-level rows or action counts.

## Evidence boundary

These are self-reported firm actions and retrospective attributions, not administrative headcount changes. The actions are not stated to be mutually exclusive and have different numbers of workers behind them, so subtracting percentages does not yield a net employment effect. "Retrained workers" is an employer action, not evidence that workers completed training or gained independent capability.
