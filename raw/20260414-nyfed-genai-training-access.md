# Use of Gen AI in the Workplace and the Value of Access to Training

> Canonical source: https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/04/use-of-gen-ai-in-the-workplace-and-the-value-of-access-to-training/

## Source locator

- Published 2026-04-14; underlying supplemental SCE survey fielded November 2025.
- Among currently employed respondents:
  - 39% report using AI at work currently or in the prior 12 months;
  - 15.9% report that their employer currently offers AI-related training.
- Respondents without training access are asked how much pay they would give up to obtain it.
- Respondents with training access are asked how much compensation they would require to lose it.
- The paper explicitly distinguishes training availability from actual participation/completion.

## Evidence boundary

Training availability is a worker-reported access measure, not a completed-training or skill-acquisition outcome. Willingness-to-pay/accept values are stated preferences, not realized wages. The survey does not link respondents to employer administrative hiring flows or independent skill tests.
