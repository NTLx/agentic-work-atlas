# Role of Human-AI Interaction in Selective Prediction

> Canonical source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06751
> Version used: v2, 2022-05-16.

## Source locator

- Uses a real-world conservation/camera-trap prediction setting.
- Manipulates two messaging components presented to a human after selective deferral:
  1. whether the human sees the AI prediction;
  2. whether the human is told that the selective system chose to defer.
- Human judgement accuracy changes significantly with messaging.
- The reported best strategy in the study informs the human that the system deferred but does not reveal the AI prediction.
- Showing an incorrect AI prediction can mislead the human.
- Authors argue composite human-AI accuracy must be evaluated with humans in the loop.

## Evidence boundary

This experiment isolates message framing more directly than most handoff studies, but it uses a fixed receiver setting and classification task. It does not manipulate queue state, receiver identity, packet completeness or long-horizon handoff timing.
