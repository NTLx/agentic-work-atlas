# How AI Impacts Skill Formation

> Canonical source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20245
> Version used: v2, 2026-02-01.

## Source locator

- Randomized experiment, main sample n=52, 26 participants per group.
- Participants have prior Python experience but are unfamiliar with the Trio library.
- Treatment group receives AI assistance during coding; control group does not.
- Coding phase lasts up to 35 minutes.
- Afterwards both groups take a no-AI comprehension test:
  - 14 questions;
  - maximum score 27.
- Reported treatment difference: AI-assisted group scores 4.15 points lower.
- Effect size d=0.738, p=0.010.
- Average task completion-time difference is not statistically significant.
- Most participants are age 25–35; only a small subset has 1–3 years of programming experience.

## Evidence boundary

The randomized design identifies a short-run skill-acquisition effect under this specific programming/library task. It does not measure long-term retention, workplace promotion, occupational qualification, employer training systems or economy-wide skill reproduction. "Unfamiliar with Trio" is not equivalent to "entry-level worker."
