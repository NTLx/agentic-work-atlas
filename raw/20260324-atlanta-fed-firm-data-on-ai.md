# Firm Data on AI

> Canonical source: https://www.atlantafed.org/-/media/Project/Atlanta/FRBA/Documents/research/publication/working-paper/2026/03/24/03-firm-data-on-ai.pdf

## Source locator

- Survey window: November 2025 through January 2026.
- Nearly 6,000 executives across the United States, United Kingdom, Germany and Australia.
- US SBU, UK DMP and German BOP-F estimates are employment-weighted; Australian BOSS is unweighted; pooled results use response-count weighting.
- Firms report AI's effect over the prior three years and their expected effect over the next three years.
- Five-category answers are mapped to numerical values 0, +/-2.5 and +/-7.5.
- Table 4 pooled employment impact:
  - retrospective reported attribution: 0.00%;
  - expected next-three-year impact: -0.68%.

## Evidence boundary

The retrospective figure is a survey attribution converted from categorical responses, not an administrative before/after headcount estimate. Future values are expectations. Different country surveys have different frames and weights. The data do not provide a shared occupation, early-career or training-outcome panel.
