# You're (not) Hired

> Canonical profile: https://lehd.ces.census.gov/applications/creat/paper-profile/1415
> Working Paper: CES-26-27.

## Source locator

- U.S. Census Center for Economic Studies Working Paper CES-26-27.
- Author: Lee Tucker.
- April 2026; Census working-paper directory lists the PDF on 2026-04-28.
- Uses detailed tabulations from matched employer–employee administrative data / Quarterly Workforce Indicators.
- Focus: early-career workers ages 22–24.
- Compares industry-state cells by AI exposure.
- Abstract reports:
  - immediate, sizable and persistent decrease in early-career hires after ChatGPT in the most AI-exposed cells;
  - decline in hires is the primary cause of subsequent employment declines;
  - regression-adjusted early-career employment in the most-exposed quintile declines about 12% over 10 quarters;
  - less-exposed industries remain comparatively stable.
- The hiring rate largely recovers by early 2025, attributed to a smaller employment base.

## Evidence boundary

The design uses industry-state exposure rather than directly observed firm AI adoption. Administrative data strongly measure hires/employment but do not reveal training, promotion or independent skill outcomes. The 12% is a regression-adjusted relative employment result for a specific early-career group and exposure design, not a universal reduction in entry-level employment.
