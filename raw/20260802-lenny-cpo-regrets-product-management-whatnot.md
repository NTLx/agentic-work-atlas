## Third-Party Summary (Aakash Gupta, 2026-08-02, Substack note)

> Source: https://substack.com/@aakashgupta/note/c-259115252
> Attribution: Aakash Gupta's paraphrase of Tom Verrilli's interview; preserved here for its concrete numbers and operating mechanics, not as primary transcript.

Whatnot's CPO: "We regret that product management exists."

- 31,832 PMs applied to Whatnot in 2 years. They hired one.
- Tom Verrilli (CPO at Twitch before this) just published a manifesto on why his own function is mostly broken. You're twice as likely to hit a hole in one as land a PM job there by applying.
- The cap is the strategy.
- Whatnot runs their entire product surface with **20 PMs across 1,200+ employees**. Tom estimates Twitch and eBay combined have over 400.
- **PMs map to problems, not engineering managers.** The senior PM building a new sales format for fashion sellers works across listings, logistics, AND payments. No single EM owns her.
- **Everybody ships.** CEO Grant LaFontaine personally got on the phone with a Zendesk support engineer when tickets started dropping. Cofounder Logan hand-onboarded the first seller in Australia. Tom himself vibecodes features as an IC.
- They hire almost exclusively senior PMs over endless alignment meetings and itching to build again. Or they convert proven sales/ops folks who already understand the customer.
- There's no distinction in titles or comp between managers and ICs at Whatnot. The PMs who do manage spend 90%+ of their time as ICs anyway.
- In 2025, Whatnot ran **750 experiments**. Roughly 3 ship/don't-ship decisions per day. Tom calculated that making each of those decisions 3 days faster compounds to **$1.1B in incremental earnings for Whatnot sellers over 2 years**.
- "Speed is the entire premise. Once it's lost, it never comes back."

## Optimixed TL;DR (third-party summary)

Source: https://www.optimixed.com/this-cpo-regrets-that-product-management-exists-tom-verrilli-cpo-of-whatnot/

- **AI's Impact on Product Roles:** AI is not just a tool but a transformative force reshaping how product managers and data scientists operate, enabling smarter decision-making and streamlined workflows.
- **Hiring Philosophy:** Tom prioritizes recruiting experienced senior ICs who can take full ownership of projects, reducing micromanagement and fostering innovation.
- **Leadership Lessons:** Drawing from his tenure at Twitch and Twitter, Tom shares that simply hiring great talent isn't sufficient; leaders must empower teams by removing obstacles and enabling autonomy.
- **Mental Models:** He employs unique frameworks like the "play the accordion" mental model to manage team dynamics and product scaling effectively.
- **Why Whatnot's Product Team Stands Out:** Founded on the premise "we regret that product management exists," Whatnot's product team rethinks the traditional role of PMs, focusing instead on leveraging AI and empowering senior contributors to deliver rapid growth and innovation. This approach has been critical in achieving over $8 billion in gross merchandise value (GMV), making Whatnot a landmark success story in live shopping platforms.
