# Labor market impacts of AI

> Canonical source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/labor-market-impacts
> Figure 7 legend corrected 2026-03-08.

## Source locator

- Uses August and November 2025 Claude usage to build occupation-level exposure.
- Exposure combines observed work/API task traffic with feasibility, automation weighting and estimated task-time shares.
- O*NET-SOC is crosswalked to CPS occupation coding.
- Young-worker analysis focuses on ages 22–25 and adjacent-month reports of new work.
- The paper reports new-work rate in high-exposure occupations roughly 14% below the 2022 level, with only marginal statistical significance.
- Exposure is occupation-level and based on Claude usage; it is not firm adoption or worker-specific tool use.

## Evidence boundary

The exposure measure contains platform selection, model classification and task-weight assumptions. It is not a pure percentage of tasks automated. The young-worker result concerns a flow rate, not headcount, layoffs, first jobs only or an employer causal effect. Firm adoption and training are not linked.
