# Canaries in the Coal Mine?

> Canonical source: https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/app/uploads/2026/08/Canaries_August2026.pdf
> Version used: August 2026 revision.

## Source locator

- Monthly ADP worker–firm payroll matches from January 2021 through June 2026.
- Roughly 3.5–5.0 million full-time, under-70, positive-earnings worker–firm matches per month.
- Firms are kept in a balanced panel.
- Occupation titles are mapped to SOC and linked to AI-exposure measures.
- Ages 22–25 in high-exposure occupations show an employment path roughly 19% below the low-exposure comparison path.
- Hiring is defined as a worker–firm match appearing this month but not the prior month; separation is the reverse.
- Flow rates use rolling 12-month counts divided by group employment 12 months earlier.

## Evidence boundary

The 19% figure is a relative employment-path gap, not a direct causal treatment effect or a layoff percentage. ADP clients and the balanced-firm panel are selected relative to the full labor market. Ages 22–25 are an early-career proxy, not a universal entry-level definition. The data do not directly observe actual employer AI adoption, training or independent skill outcomes.
