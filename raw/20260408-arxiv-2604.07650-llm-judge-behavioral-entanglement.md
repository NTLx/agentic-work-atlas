# A Statistical Framework for Auditing Behavioral Dependence and Induced Bias in LLM Judges

> Canonical source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07650
> Version used: v2, revised 2026-08-09.

## Source locator

- Abstract / main results: 18 LLMs from six model families.
- Metrics: Difficulty-Weighted Behavioral Entanglement Index (BEI) and Cumulative Information Gain (CIG).
- MMLU-Pro transfer: judge over-endorsement association, BEI ρ=0.508 and CIG ρ=0.520, both p<0.01.
- MATH-500 transfer: BEI ρ=0.441 and CIG ρ=0.457, both p<0.05.
- De-entangled verifier ensemble reweighting: +3.5 percentage points accuracy and +2.6 percentage points precision versus majority voting.

## Evidence boundary

The paper measures behavioral dependence and its association with judge bias. It does not isolate the full engineering notion of verifier independence across objective, evidence, execution, and time, and it does not prove that shared training lineage is the unique cause of correlated errors.
